## 0. Bottom line

1. **578 and 744 contain no same-text candidate for P1030680.**
   - No decrypt with a 1 May TOO between 12:00 and 16:30 comes from the Thetis community (FdU Ausb / Captain (U/B) Training, 4./5./25./26./31. Flott, TF boats, Agru-Front, UAK, Otto Wünsche, training U-boats).
   - The Thetis-community decrypts of 1 May that exist are either before noon (already known from 577) or after the 16:30 reception (1638, 1707, 1803, 2106, 2206, 2221).
   - The only **1 May** decrypts with a TOO within ±2 minutes of the form's `?552/?553` are:
     - NLO Blankenese → MOK Nord, 1554, 4835 kc/s;
     - 577_4_p070, Naval Chief Command Baltic, TOO 0553.
   - Both are the wrong community and far too long.
2. **Calibration (new): Bletchley's TOI is GMT; TOO is German time (GMT+2).**
   - 577_5_p004: TOI 1034, which is 12:34 German. U-534 received P1030668 at 12:33.
   - 578_03_p237: TOI 1121, which is 13:21 German. U-534 received P1030673 at 13:21.
   - So if Bletchley had copied P1030680's own NB transmission, it would carry a TOI of about 1430/1/5 on 4860 kc/s. No such item exists. The 1 May 4860 items in 578 are TOO 1143, 1803 and 2106.
   - This absence is expected. Thetis was never broken, so the Thetis transmission itself could never become a ZTPG decrypt. Only a copy in a broken key could. The absence also shows that Bletchley decrypted only a fraction of 1 May afternoon traffic. It is not evidence that no copy existed.
3. **The best thematic lead is p179, Captain (U/B) Training U.1 → Front Line Training Group Bornholm (Agru-Front), TOO 1707:** "confirm the transfer of 'Peter von Danzig' to Warnemünde; report departure".
   - Its community is exactly Thetis (FdU Ausb ↔ Agru-Front on Bornholm).
   - Its length is roughly right (62 English letters ≈ 81 German).
   - But its TOO is 37 minutes *after* P1030680 was received, so at best it is a fragment source: an earlier Thetis message on the same subject, or a later re-send.
   - It was tested as full text and as fragments (§4). **Result: null.**
4. The 2 May Thetis-community pages fail on date. P1030680's form date is 1.5.45, and serial 70 belongs to the 1 May NB sequence. This includes p076: TOO **1153**, Captain (U/B) Training → FdU Ost, 25./26. Flott, "the following torpedo recovery vessels will be laid up". The page image shows TOI 1319/**2**/5/45.

## 3. Reconstructions

Only p179 qualifies even as a thematic source. Neither TOO allows a straight same-text copy, and the other candidates fail on net, community, date or length.

For p179 the German is reconstructed with the Rosetta rules from research/13 §3. "CONFIRM" is either the infinitive-imperative `BESTAETIGEN` or FdU confirming a proposal (`…BESTAETIGT`, `GENEHMIGT`, `BESTAETIGE…`). "Report departure" is `AUSLAUFEN MELDEN`. Ships are J-quoted.

**Components:**
- address: `AGRUFRONT`, `AGRUXFRONT`, `(X)BORNHOLM`, or none;
- sender: `VONVONFXDXUUUAUSB`, `VVVFXDXUUUAUSB`, or none;
- `UEBERFUEHRUNG` / `VERLEGUNG`;
- `JPETERVONDANZIGJ` / `PETERVONDANZIG`;
- `NACHWARNEMUENDE`;
- `BESTAETIGEN` / `BESTAETIGT` / `WIRDBESTAETIGT` / `GENEHMIGT`, or verb first `BESTAETIGE…`;
- `X`, `XX` or nothing;
- `AUSLAUFENMELDEN`.

**Letter counts:**
- The shortest full body is `VERLEGUNGPETERVONDANZIGNACHWARNEMUENDEGENEHMIGTAUSLAUFENMELDEN`, 62 letters.
- Any address plus `XX` gives ≥ 73. Any FdU sender block gives ≥ 76.
- 86 variants fall in 60–72 letters. **Only 6 are collision-free at offset 0, and all are unaddressed**:

**The 29–31-letter core cannot be run at k=1.**
- At k=1 each of its two blocks has only 1–3 loops.
- A benchmark on 8 wheel orders gave 117 M block stops. Extrapolated, that is about 18 h at 2 threads and far past the ranking cap.
- So the core runs at k=0, which gives 3–8 loops and 0 stops in the same benchmark.
- The 38–44-letter forms run at k=1.

## 4. Bombe tests

**Setup:**
- `tools/bombe/bombe` over the full space: B/C thin × Beta/Gamma × 336 orders.
- Middle ring pinned (about 80% of middle-ring cases for full-length cribs, about 94% for cribs in the first 35 letters).
- `--excl 1,10,20,25,32,58,60,67,68,71`, `--threads 2` (resource limit).
- Survivors ranked by climb with `navalblend_quadgrams`. The acceptance test is post-climb `crib_mismatch ≤ k` together with a German-looking rest.

## 5. What remains

1. **A known TOO.** the human reader’s reading `?552/?553` combined with Bletchley's ±1 minute gives a 1551–1554 window. No home-waters decrypt from the community has that TOO in 577, 578, 743 or 744.
   - The next ZTPG volumes (DEFE 3/579 onward, ZTPG 369000+, issued after 17 May) could still hold late-decrypted 1 May afternoon traffic.
   - The high-resolution form photo remains the cheapest way to fix the TOO.
2. **Bletchley decrypted only part of 1 May afternoon traffic.**
   - From 12:00–16:30 German time, 578 has about 20 home-waters decrypts. None is from FdU Ausb or the training flotillas.
   - FdU Ausb's own traffic appears at 1123 (577), then at 1638 (Offizier) and 1707.
   - So a same-text copy may simply never have been decrypted. That is a limit of the source, not a negative result.
3. The p179 family (Agru-Front, Peter von Danzig) is closed as worded (§4), within the stated limits: pinned middle ring, k, and adjacency of ship name and destination.
4. Families that are still open and have no crib source: 26. Flottille movements out of Warnemünde (Soviet thrust on Rostock at 12:00, Warnemünde under fire from 15:15), and FdU Ausb orders to TF boats in the afternoon (TF 18 to Neustadt).
