GIST Five German U-boats (U.3002, U.3013, U.2528, U.2533, U.3018) despatched to 3rd U/Boat Instructional Division on 29 April 1945 with personnel and armament details.

C59

TO: I D 8 G

FROM: N S

4282 KC/S        TOO 1145                TOI 1144/29/4/45

FROM: 25TH U/BOAT FLOTILLA

TO: ADMIRAL CU/BD

THE FOLLOWING WERE DESPATCHED TO THE 3RD U/BOAT INSTRUCTIONAL
DIVISION ON 29/4/45:

1) U.3002 WITH 5 OFFICERS, 4 SUBORDINATE OFFICERS, 13 PETTY OFFICERS,
34 MEN, FOUR 2 CH GUNS, 2000 ROUNDS, 8 PISTOLS.

2) U.3013 WITH 5 OFFICERS, 2 SUBORDINATE OFFICERS, 16 PETTY OFFICERS,
33 MEN, FIVE 2 CH GUNS, 1300 ROUNDS, 9 PISTOLS.

3) U.2528 WITH 5 OFFICERS, 3 SUBORDINATE OFFICERS, 12 PETTY OFFICERS,
27 MEN, FOUR 2 CH GUNS, 2000 ROUNDS, 5 PISTOLS AND 2 STAR
SIGNAL PISTOLS.

4) U.2533 WITH 4 OFFICERS, 1 MIDSHIPMAN, 3 SUBORDINATE OFFICERS,
16 PETTY OFFICERS, 29 MEN, FIVE 2 CH GUNS, 1200 ROUNDS, 5 PISTOLS.

5) U.3018 WITH 6 OFFICERS, 2 SUBORDINATE OFFICERS, 16 PETTY OFFICERS,
32 MEN, FOUR 2 CH GUNS, 600 ROUNDS, 7 PISTOLS.
