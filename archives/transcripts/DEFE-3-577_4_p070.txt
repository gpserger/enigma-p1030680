GIST Naval Chief Command Baltic issues orders regarding demolition and mining of German Baltic ports west of Swinemuende.

FROM NS

368 KCS    TOO 0553    TOI 0613/1/5/45

FROM: NAVAL CHIEF COMMAND BALTIC

TO  : N.O.I.C. SASSNITZ
     N.O.I.C. STRALSUND

MOST IMMEDIATE    MOST SECRET 2047.17

1) IN ALL GERMAN BALTIC PORTS WEST OF SWINEMUENDE, HARBOUR INSTALLATIONS ARE NOW ONLY TO BE CRIPPLED IF THREATENED BY THE ANGLO-AMERICANS. NOT ONLY DEMOLITION OF QUAY WALLS, HARBOUR EQUIPMENT ETC., BUT MINING ALSO, MUST NOT BE CARRIED OUT . IN THE EVENT OF A RUSSIAN ADVANCE TO THE WEST, BLOCKING AS WELL AS CRIPPLING.

2) DEMOLITION AND MINING OF DANISH PORTS ONLY IN THE CASE OF ENEMY LANDING OPERATIONS AGAINST THE DANISH AREA, OTHERWISE, AS IN PARA. 1, EXCEPT FOR THE WEST JUTLAND HARBOURS. THESE ARE TO BE DESTROYED IN ALL CIRCUMSTANCES ACCORDING TO PREVIOUS ORDERS
