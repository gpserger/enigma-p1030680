5TH U/B FLOTILLA

1) EXAMINATION IN DOCK OF U.2927 SHEWS THAT HER PRESSURE HULL ON
THE PORT SIDE HAS BEEN DEEPLY DENTED BETWEEN THE FRAMES FOR
TWO-THIRDS OF ITS LENGTH AND HALF ITS HEIGHT. SO THE BOAT IS NO
LONGER CAPABLE OF BEING USED FOR FRONT-LINE OPERATIONS. IT WILL
BE IMPOSSIBLE TO RESTORE THE BOATS PREVIOUS STRUCTURAL STRENGTH
AND SHE HAS BEEN RELEASED FOR BREAKING-UP. PROPOSE SHE BE PAID
OFF FORTHWITH.

2) DAMAGE OBVIOUSLY CAUSED BY A/C BOMBS BUT WAS NOT MENTIONED AT
DOCKYARD CONFERENCE. THE CREW TRANSFERRED TO U.2369 WHICH SAILED
ON 2/4/45.

[handwritten] 0355/1/5/45 CEL/EB
