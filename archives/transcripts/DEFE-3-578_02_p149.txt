GIST Detailed order for U-boat U 2503 escort operation: ground mine escort via specified waypoints, diving instructions near Schwarz 14, and passage procedures to Frederikshavn with specific speed and course requirements.

FROM: 8TH DEFENCE DIVISION
TO: M 406 (ACKNOWLEDGE RECEIPT)

GO TO ANCHORAGE 'C', AT 2200 THERE GROUND MINE ESCORT FOR U 2503
VIA WAY 'WEIDE', 28, 36, 48 TO 'BLUETE', PART COMPANY THERE.
ADJUST NAVIGATION SO THAT SCHWARZ 03 IS NOT PASSED BEFORE 0200.
U-BOAT IS TO EMBARK AN OFFICER FROM GUARD SHIP ANCHORAGE 'E'.
ON ARRIVAL AT SCHWARZ 14 U-BOAT IS TO BOTTOM ON DIVING POSITION,
WHICH IS KNOWN TO GUARDSHIP. ONWARD PASSAGE TO 'BLUETE' ACCORDING TO AIR SITUATION, ABOUT 0200/4/5. DURING DIVING TIME M 406
TO PROCEED UNDER LEE OF ANHOLT. AFTER PARTING WITH CONVOY TO
FREDERIKSHAVN. ADHERE STRICTLY TO WAYS. SPEED OF U-BOAT WITH
SWEEP 11 KNOTS. DRAUGHT OF U-BOAT 7.5 METRES

0350/6/5/45+EGT/AM
