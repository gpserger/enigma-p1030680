FROM: NAVAL WAR STAFF
TO:   ADMIRAL NETHERLANDS

MOST IMMEDIATE        MOST SECRET

1.  NAVAL CHIEF COMMAND BALTIC:
    IN THE EASTERN BALTIC, THERE IS.IN HELA OWING TO LACK OF
    SHIPPING SPACE AN ACCUMULATION OF 27000 NON-COMBATANTS.
    ALL QUIET ON THE HELA LAND FRONT.    IN THE WESTERN [handwritten] ■■
    BALTIC AREA, THE ENEMY ON THE DIEVENOW AND PEENE FRONT
    HAS.LANDED ON USEDOM AND WOLLIN.    W/T STATION. SWINEMUENDE
    ANNOUNCED ITS IMPENDING CLOSING DOWN ON THE AFTERNOON OF
    4/5.    THE FEXXX DEVELOPMENT OF.THE SITUATION ON RUEGEN
    IS STILL OBSCURE.    RUSSIAN DEMANDS FOR A CAPITULATION
    WERE MET BY THE ISLAND COMMANDANT WITH A REQUEST FOR 24
    HOURS TO THINK THE MATTER OVER.
