MOST IMMEDIATE                                          MOST SECRET

THE HEAVY DIESEL OIL SITUATION, MAKES IT NECESSARY, IF NO FURTHER
SUPPLIES ARE TO ARRIVE, TO SECURE ALL STOCKS OF HEAVY DIESEL OIL
FOR THE CONDUCT OF THE BATTLE IN THE FORTRESS OF HOLLAND. I HAVE
THEREFORE DECLARED THE HEAVY DEXXX DIESEL OIL STOCKS OF ADMIRAL
NETHERLANDS AND S.O.S-BOATS, TOTALLING 324 CBM., TO BE THE
UNTOUCHABLE RESERVE OF THE FORTRESS WITH EFFECT FROM 27/4. C. IN
C. OF THE NAVY IS REQUESTED TO RELEASE THESE STOCKS.
