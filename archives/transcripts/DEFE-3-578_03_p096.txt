CHANNEL ISLANDS' DAY'S REPORT:

1) TRANSFER OF A GUN-CARRIER GUERNSEY-JERSEY. C IN C WITH AN M-BOAT
JERSEY-GUERNSEY. ENGAGEMENT WITH A LIBERATOR EN ROUTE. THE
M-BOAT WAS HIT ON THE RAIL, ONE MAN SLIGHTLY WOUNDED. TWO HITS
OBSERVED IN THE FUSELAGE OF THE LIBERATOR. THE M-BOAT CONTINUED
TO ALEXXX ALDERNEY WITH 3 TONS OF CREAM CHEESE. ONE HS-BOAT TOOK
SUPPLIES JERSEY-MINQUIERS AND BACK.

2) SIGHTINGS: AT 1115 IN BF 3552, ONE ESCORT DESTROYER IN WAITING
POSITION; AT 1840 IN THE SAME SQUARE, ONE ESCORT DESTROYER, COURSE
NORTH .

3) FROM 1231 TO 1250, HIGH-LEVEL RECCE OF JERSEY AND GUERNSEY BY A
SPITFIRE. FROM 1532 TO 1543, ONE UNIDENTIFIED A/C OVER JERSEY.
FROM 1712 TO 1743, ONE LIBERATOR ON LOW-LEVEL SEA RECCE BETWEEN
GUERNSEY AND JERSEY. (ENGAGED BY M-BOAT.)
