GIST Naval authority reports on agreement for supply ships to enter Rotterdam from 4/5, with details on ship markings, armament, and escort arrangements.

FROM: ADMIRAL NETHERLANDS
TO: NAVAL CHIEF COMMAND NORTH

FURTHER TO W/T MESSAGE 2358 OF 30/4 THE FOLLOWING IS REPORTED:
IN ACCORDANCE WITH AN GREEMENT REACHED YESTERDAY BETWEEN THE
REICH COMMISSAR AND THE ALLIES, 3 SUPPLY SHIPS AT A TIME WILL
ENTER ROTTERDAM REGULARLY AS FROM 4/5, PROBABLY AT INTERVALS OF
3 DAYS. MARKINGS OF ALL VESSELS CONSIST OF VERTICAL WHITE
STRIPES ON THE SHIPS' HULLS AND SHIPS WILL PROCEED LIT UP. SHIPS
ARE IN PART ARMED, GUNS IN SECURED POSITION. CONVOYS WILL BE
ESCORTED AS FAR AS THE R/V BY ENGLISH ESCORT VESSELS. REQUEST
B.D.U. AND K.D.K. BE INFORMED. DETAILED REPORT WILL FOLLOW AFTER
FINAL CONSULTATIONS ON 2/5.

2347/5/5/45 EE/MJU
