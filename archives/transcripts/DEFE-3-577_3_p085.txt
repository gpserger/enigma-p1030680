IN THIS HARDEST HOUR IN THE FIGHT FOR THE FATE OF GERMANY, THE
FUEHRER THINKS OF HIS SOLDIERS IN THE FORTRESSES ON THE XX
ATLANTIC AND IN THE AEGEAN AND EXPECTS THAT THEY WILL CONTINUE
TO FULFIL THEIR DUTY WITH EXEMPLARY SOLDIERLY BEARING, DESIST
FROM ANY INDEPENDENT ACTION, AND ONLY ACT IN ACCORDANCE WITH
ORDERS ISSUED THROUGH THE SUPREME COMMAND OF THE ARMED FORCES.
