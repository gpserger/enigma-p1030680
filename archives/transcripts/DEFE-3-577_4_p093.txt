GIST Admiral Eastern Baltic's evening sitrep for 28/4 reports an MTB attack on Hela Roads torpedoing the wreck of S.S. Emilie Sauber and artillery bombardment of Libau town, canal and winter harbour.

FROM NS

4412 KCS    TOO 1830/28    TOI 0455/29/4/45

FROMX : ADMIRAL EASTERN BALTIC

TO  : DISTRIBUTION FOR SITUATION REPORTS

EVENING SITREP 28/4:

1) AT 0230 DURING MTB ATTACK ON HELA ROADS THE WRECK OF S.S.
'EMILIE SAUBER' WAS TORPEDOED. SALVAGE ABANDONED. MTB'S ENGAGED
BY BATTERIES AND ROADSTEAD PATROL. THE ENEMY TURNED AWAY WHEN FIRED
ON, MAKING SMOKE.

2) LATVIA: SITUATION A.M. 28/4:
LIBAU ARTILLERY BOMBARDMENT DURING THE AFTERNOON AND EVENING ON
TOWN CANAL, WINTER HARBOUR AND CENTRE OF TOWN. SLIGHT DAMAGE TO
BUILDINGS.
