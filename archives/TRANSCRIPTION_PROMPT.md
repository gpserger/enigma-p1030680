# Transcription instructions (Bletchley Park ZTPG decrypts, DEFE 3/577, 1945)

For each assigned page image in /opt/enigma-p1030680/archives/pages/ (files named like
DEFE-3-577_3_p-042.png), read the image with the Read tool and write ONE file
/opt/enigma-p1030680/archives/transcripts/<volume>_p<page>.txt, e.g. DEFE-3-577_3_p042.txt.

File format:
Line 1:  META serial=<ZTPG number or ?> too=<T O O time(s) or ?> toi=<T O I hhmm/d/m/45 or ?> freq=<kc/s or ?> from=<sender or ?> to=<addressee or ?>
Line 2:  GIST <one English sentence: who to whom about what>
Line 3:  blank
Line 4+: faithful transcription of the whole page, preserving line breaks. Typed text exactly as
         printed (keep XX corrections, ((double parentheses)), etc.). Handwritten marks in
         [square brackets, marked "handwritten"]. Unreadable characters as ?. Do not guess.
Skip the printed archive caption and the stamped folio number.

Process pages one at a time. Do not read any other files. Do not analyse; transcribe.
When finished, reply with: pages done, pages you could not read well (and why), and any page
whose GIST mentions U-boats, training flotillas, Travemünde, Kiel, Fehmarn, Norway transfer,
FdU, Kommandierender Admiral der U-Boote, or orders "to all" units.
